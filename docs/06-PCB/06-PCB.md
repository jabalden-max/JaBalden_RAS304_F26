## Overview

This is a 2-layer, 98 × 98 mm KiCad PCB titled “JB TEAM 105”, 1.6 mm thick. It's a small power and interface board for a microcontroller module. The power section has an input connector and fuse, a protection diode, an inductor, and several regulator ICs. Several small connectors and eight test points provide the interfaces. Four long rows of 2.54 mm pin headers on the right side likely break out the module’s pins. The rest is a switch, LEDs, about 80 SMD resistors and capacitors, and 11 vias.
